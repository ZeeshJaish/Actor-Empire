# Task 9 — Forecasts and shared evidence

The Greenlight attention signal and the release preview now consume cinema inputs. Opening scenarios run the same pure market kernel with visible delivery and bounded audience-intent uncertainty. They cannot settle money or expose hidden final quality. Country/chain choice supplies access; paid campaigns and announcements supply awareness. Current competition is included; later unannounced competition remains uncertain.

Calculation runs on request in a Web Worker. Relevant changes cancel stale work; ordinary draft autosaves do not cancel or recompute a forecast. Before calculation, money is unavailable rather than a fabricated zero. Opening producer receipts are labelled opening scenarios, not lifetime value. Calendar and cinema campaign screens no longer display separate hype-derived rank/profit/bid promises.

Financial panels use getCinemaProjectFinance for gross, collected receipts across media, paid costs, investors/backend, cost recovery and profit. Signed actor backend uses the title's committed ledger; an absent total actor-pay record is not displayed as zero. IMDb adds paid attendance/repeat, discovery, earned familiarity and known-window waiting evidence with minimum thresholds. Generic unconverted interest is not called a streaming wait. Numerical IMDb calculations remain unchanged.

Compact audience signals are retained at cinema close for reports and later quotes. Legacy historical attendance remains unknown or explicitly partial. Signals summarize simulated audience behavior; they are not extra demand multipliers or audited real-world viewers.
