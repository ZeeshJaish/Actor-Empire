## Hit/loss accounting

Small means production <= $25M. **152/219 small films recovered theatrical costs (69.4%)**: 52 strong financial hits, 94 other profits, 17 near break-even and 56 clear losses. Exactly 67 have any negative theatrical result, including negative near-break-even cases. Player cinema credits recovered **11/15 closed films (73.3%)**, with 9 strong hits, 2 other profits, 1 near break-even and 3 clear losses. No closed cost denominator is unknown.

Theatrical profit is producer receipts minus production, paid campaign, booking and paid talent/backend. Exhibitor/distributor deductions are already outside producer receipts; modern revenue-share bookings do not incur a second fixed show fee. Investor ownership distributions are not counted as a second project cost. Actor fee/backend and personal cash are separate from producer profit. Open runs remain interim. A strong hit means profit >= 60% of production budget; near break-even is within 5% of total tracked costs and does not conceal exact deficits.
