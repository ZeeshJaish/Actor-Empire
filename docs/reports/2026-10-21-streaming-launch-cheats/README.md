# Streaming launch developer shortcuts and Operations analysis

Implemented two developer-menu actions in HomePage, with App-level shared processing lock and persisted-before-published saves. A durable pre-cheat checkpoint preserves pending career edits before preparation or fast-forward starts.

- Ready to Commission: prepares an uncommissioned company for 197 selected, filed and QA-approved opening markets. Adds three identified QA global subscription licences and a delivered QA Original/slate without changing existing rights contracts. Uses a subscription test offer and organic campaign, builds a funded regional physical/cloud drawing, runs actual routing/capacity rehearsal, saves configuration-bound evidence, and checkpoints the blueprint. It does not commission, launch or advance the calendar. Existing non-QA contract fields and localization assets are preserved. It replaces the unbuilt test configuration. Fresh companies receive incorporation cash, company test treasury and the exact filing-energy allowance needed.
- Fast-forward commissioned build: requires a commissioned pre-launch setup, determines the real completion date (including scheduled marketing), processes normal game weeks, persists each week before publication, yields between weeks, and retains manual Opening Night. Repeats at the completed date do not advance time. Saves that fail stop further processing; a career death also stops the action. At most 52 weeks are supported per run.

Verification: fresh and existing-company preparation; 197 real market records; launch-definition milestones and all pre-commission Build milestones; original source immutability and unchanged serialized contracts; bounded additive QA language assets; repeat preparation; real commission; 15 full processGameWeek calls with serialized reload between weeks; READY_TO_OPEN and canLaunch=true; no automatic launch; failed-save/progress ordering; repeated completion. The frozen marketplace fixture includes a deliberately partial studio Business object; that invalid stub was excluded only for the weekly lifecycle test. It is not a production business migration or repair.

Browser verification used a disposable memory-only HQ. Actual Build showed 14/14 checks ready, enabled Commission, and entered the 15-week Opening Programme. The real Anshu career was used only to inspect developer controls, with no cheat executed.

Localization limitation: canonical persistence currently retains 320 per-title language assets. These shortcuts preserve real assets and do not forge global localization quality or rewrite real licences to cover more territories. All 197 markets have a playable QA subscription offer; language quality warnings can remain on the real library. This is a separate Operations data-model gap, not a commission-gate bypass.

These Operations changes are proposed, not implemented in this task.
