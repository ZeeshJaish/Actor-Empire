# Normal-career box-office verification — 23 October 2026

Four fresh actor careers were simulated through actual App action callbacks and `processGameWeek`, without grants to cash, craft, cast, awareness or audience. All four used the identical captured executable on the `205a93c` baseline. The current shared checkout also contains separate actor-reward changes; those later changes are **not** included in these career rates. No gameplay balance change or earning cap was introduced by this verification task.

Seed2 ended from an untreated Respiratory Complication, with positive cash. The basic actor policy used ordinary wellbeing/checkup activities but did not buy condition-specific Health Clinic treatment. This outcome was retained, with no replacement or resurrection. There were 405 released-film observations, zero unknown-cost completed titles and 120 unfinished runs; unfinished titles are excluded from final economic denominators. Across worlds, 147 canonical title IDs and 176 distinct package identities recur, so observations are correlated. These are not independent player-population probabilities.

**Recovery** means producer theatrical receipts cover production, campaign, booking and paid actor/talent backend. Financing ownership distributions are not charged twice. **Financial hit** means theatrical project profit reaches 60% of production budget, matching the existing NPC financial hurdle. **Near break-even** means an exact surplus or deficit within 5% of paid costs. The 18 near-break-even films comprise 13 actual deficits and 5 surpluses; the latter are already among the 66 recovered films. Small films have 4 below-cost near-break-even titles and 1 surplus already counted among the 15 recoveries. No deficit is relabeled as recovered.

The first 104-week release cohort has89–104 additional follow-up weeks per career and no unfinished films: **47/155 recovered overall; small films 12/25 (48%)**. This differs from the all-completed sample, so a single headline recovery percentage should not be used as a fixed balancing target.

## What the results show

These small heterogeneous groups support checking quality and budget together; they do not establish causal success probabilities. Small films have viable profitable paths, while weak releases can lose money. The main remaining economy concern is larger-budget theatrical recovery: **9/71 films above $80M recovered; none reached the financial-hit hurdle**. Overall results remain loss-heavy. This is not evidence that all weak films should receive an earnings multiplier, or that all-media profitability has failed: future streaming, soundtrack and merchandise receipts are outside this theatrical report.

Five player movie credits completed: Sun Calling earned **$183.29M gross / $27.28M theatrical profit**; The Spirit of Horizon **$19.17M / −$0.30M**; Violet Dance **$48.07M / −$8.08M**; Alliance Calling **$16.72M / −$2.74M**; Ash Asking **$5.25M / −$0.96M**. Four of those packages delivered quality 23–33; Sun Calling delivered 62.5. This is a small fixed-policy actor sample, not a universal player win rate. Some other credits were series, streaming or unfinished.

No ordinary observed release crossed $1B, $2B or $3B. The highest reached **$343.01M**. Zero exceptional films in four early careers establishes that they were not automatic here; it does not estimate a universal rarity rate or prove attainability.

Completed runs naturally closed after 26–59 weeks (median 40);95% of lifetime gross arrived in 10–21 weeks (median 14). Long low-revenue tails are visible and should remain separate from the earning ceiling question.

## Safety and resource limits

The normal cohort performed **561 verified generation writes, 73 explicit reload/migrations, 817 duplicate-week settlement rejections**, and one failed-promotion recovery per career. Wallet/time/screen supply bounds and every weekly split of gross into producer, exhibitor and distribution receipts were asserted. All tracked weekly gross reconciled to saved lifetime gross. Existing story identities, cash, balances, signed casting terms and previously paid cinema records survived migration. Only previously missing AUTO story defaults were allowed to normalize.

One owned simulation worker ran at a time. Its measured peak maxRSS was **1832.61MiB**; the final 1GB-heap controller peaked around1564MiB for its worker tree. System swap-growth guards paused two earlier batches; completed checkpoints survived and were resumed. The3GiB process-tree ceiling, minimum 15% system-free check and maximum 512MiB swap growth per batch stayed enabled. No unrelated processes were terminated.

## Conditional advanced-owner check

The older earned-precursor snapshot was found to contain an already-deceased owner. Its cinema records are market evidence, but it is rejected as active-owner gameplay proof. The replacement probe starts from the explicitly authored funded-owner fixture, earns four precursors through 104 actual weeks with paid healthcare, then forks the same alive checkpoint and RNG/clock into strong/weak finales. These conditional cases are excluded from ordinary-career rarity rates. The owner starts with authored $3B studio / $1M personal cash and prepared completed craft/cast packages; this does not prove normal-founder affordability or production execution.

Both finales debit **$300M production + $350M campaign** through the actual funding/exposure path and face a common world slate of24–31 films:

The strong film earned95% of its **observed** gross by week32; this is not a lifetime percentile. The weak film earned95% of final lifetime gross by week12. Both owners remain alive. Paid cash debits, four earned precursor audiences, full weekly gross reconciliation, duplicate settlement rejection and failed-save recovery were verified. A large cast and successful universe can support a major opening while weak delivery still produces a costly failure.

**$2–3B-range attainment is demonstrated at$2.314B; exact$3B, the strong film's lifetime total and its natural closing date remain unverified.** Its saved state remains open, without a forced exit or earning cap. Four system-wide swap-growth guard pauses prevented further observation; a last512MB audit attempt exhausted its worker heap before advancing the protected checkpoint. No game/save assertion failed and the already completed normal/weak results were retained. Further heavy runs are stopped to honor the memory constraint; this report does not claim complete blockbuster-tail validation.
