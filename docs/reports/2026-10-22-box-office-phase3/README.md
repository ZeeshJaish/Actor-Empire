## Audience behavior

Awareness gates first-time intent. Genre, local language, access, affordability, leisure time, earned attachment, universe familiarity and known streaming windows shape the request. A request is not a paid admission. DEFER contains interest and zero requested admissions, reserves no money and grants no streaming rights or playback access.

Actual delivered experience earns overlapping actor/studio favorites. Both the old and current role matter; duplicate cast identities and character credits are merged. Repeated disappointing experiences can weaken attachment. Universe buildup uses earlier observed viewing and satisfaction, meaningful returning characters, payoff, accessibility and cadence. No mandatory precursor count is imposed. Compacted universe memory survives retirement of detailed predecessor matrices.

Satisfied seen viewers can request a repeat with declining propensity after recent repeat visits. Word of mouth consumes a finite lifetime contact budget from actual satisfied viewers, reaches only net-new aware people, and respects local fit. The same unchanged audience cannot mint a fresh contact budget indefinitely.

The read-only world adapter uses canonical population/cohorts and participation's cinema-only wallet. Streaming allocations stay separate. Canonical player/NPC project IDs connect WE6 observed viewing to cast and universe history. Gross, catalogue presence, subscribers and starts do not become a lifetime unique-viewer ledger. Missing historical theater counts remain unknown. WE6 catch-up is explicitly a conservative estimate from the smaller of observed accounts/viewers, distributed among the country's cohorts; completion is a satisfaction proxy only when measured title satisfaction is unavailable.
