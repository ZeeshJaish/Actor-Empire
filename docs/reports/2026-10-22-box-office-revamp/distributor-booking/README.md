# Existing distributor booking: implemented and desktop-verified

Tuning 3 uses the existing distributors, country audience/economy and rival platforms. There is no hard gross ceiling, budget-return guarantee, hit quota or forced maximum theatrical age. Worldwide access does not create awareness or paid intent.

Existing chain screens are partitioned consistently across the complete country registry. Local openings use public demand; renewals use actual attendance, contribution and occupancy. Real screen-days and audience funding are shared across films. Reachable home alternatives use prior committed customers/viewing and canonical country/plan/title permissions, without creating customers, views or money.

Historical receipts, cash, closed titles and signed obligations survive migration. Player-studio, offered-role and NPC films retain the common exactly-once settlement. A real IndexedDB player/manifest snapshot race and sparse legacy-producer import edge case are fixed without disabling integrity checks or recovery.

## Measured outcomes

**76 conditional runs / 19 packages / 4 seeds / 197 countries / 2,363 cohorts / 2,383 focal title-weeks.** All 52 paired tendencies pass; all focal runs close naturally before the observation horizon. Four additional occupancy replays reproduce the original economic trajectories exactly.

Amounts are USD millions. Profit is collected theatrical producer receipts minus production, campaign and booking costs. These are conditional engine archetypes, not player-wide hit rates or project-specific forecasts. Event language/urgency assumptions and the live-adapter distinction are explicit in [the full calibration](calibration.md).

Unused wider access produces **exactly the same gross** in the matched small-film control and charges more booking cost. Strong low-exposure films can still fail to recoup; cheap weak films can sometimes profit. Outcomes are not forced into hit/flop quotas.

Observed final local closures range from week 3 to 44. Paid examples earn 95% of gross by weeks 12–20. In the seed-0 earned event, the opening has 1,037 booked pools; week 14 has 106; the final week has one quiet pool and seven shows. This is not a 44-week wide release.
