# Billion-dollar gameplay calibration — verification in progress

This follow-up checks the actual advanced-owner release path, rather than treating the absence of a gross cap or a migrated historical $3.5B total as proof of new earnings. Final attainability numbers are pending the completed actual game-loop runs.

## Changes under verification

- Foreign-language preference was applied to physical reach and ticket utility, turning the existing 30% preference into 9% conversion. It now applies once to first-visit utility. There are no automatically granted dubs or translations.
- Satisfied existing actor/studio/universe viewers can form a more attentive, higher-intent segment within the reached audience. This uses actual prior viewing, conservative overlap and current-film viewer depletion. Fame alone creates no favorite audience, and loyalty creates no awareness, people, wallets, screens or money.
- Tuning 4 upgrades future calculations from known tuning 1/2/3. Old receipts, IDs, cash, audience matrices, contracts and closed local runs remain paid facts.
- The multi-film path reproduced a pre-existing Greenlight funding bug: whole-dollar flooring discarded previously earned cents. Funding now preserves studio and production-fund cents. Dedicated red/green regressions cover this.

## Gates

1. New $1B+ earnings through real release admission, `processGameWeek`, full-world competition, settlement and verified saves.
2. Demonstrate a rare $2–3B package with earned audience; do not increase every film's conversion or force a hit quota.
3. Contrast strong/weak delivery, standalone/universe buildup, small focused/wide packages, unknown worldwide access and crowded calendars.
4. Keep paid-admission, audience, wallet, time, physical-show and financial conservation; replay/reload pays zero again.
5. Verify old paid tuning-3 saves upgrade/reload without rewriting history. Run affected tests, funding/Greenlight/save regressions, typecheck and build.

The actual-career fixture explicitly starts from an authored $3B advanced studio and completed craft packages. No previous gross, unique cinema viewers or universe familiarity is injected. The four precursors earn their attendance in actual game weeks. This verifies the genuine premiere-to-market-to-save path; it does not prove a new $2,000 career can immediately fund these packages, or measure a population-wide probability of a rare hit.
