## What changed and why

1. **One language preference.** Foreign-language preference had reduced physical reach and ticket utility, turning a 30% preference into 9% conversion. It now applies once to first-visit utility. There are no automatically granted dubs or translations; language still affects preference and recommendation diffusion.
2. **Earned loyal intent.** Satisfied prior actor/studio/universe viewers can be more attentive and more likely to plan a visit within the reached audience. Conservative overlap, meaningful cast-role weights and current-film viewer depletion remain. Fame alone grants no favorites. Loyalty creates no awareness, people, wallets, seats or money. The casual weekly intent rate remains 0.08; a reached earned loyal segment can use 0.32.
3. **Observed exceptional response can sustain interest.** Only a real paid sample and excellent delivered satisfaction extend interest's half-life. Unseen final quality and tiny samples cannot buy staying power. An independent review caught new awareness diluting the earned hold; a failing regression reproduced it, then the confidence denominator was changed to the bounded paying market. Extra awareness cannot erase an existing review sample.
4. **Future-only tuning migration.** Tuning 1/2/3 saves upgrade to 4 without rewriting historical cash, IDs, receipts, signed obligations, viewer matrices or closed local runs.
5. **Greenlight keeps earned cents.** The real second-film funding path reproduced a $0.78 loss from whole-dollar flooring. Funding now preserves studio and production-fund cents; dedicated regressions also cover a $0.37 fund remainder.
6. **Index earned affinities once.** A late-save profile found repeated scans of the entire world's affinity history inside each title/cohort/cast lookup. A cohort index removes that repeated work while retaining ordering and exact outputs.
7. **Keep paid facts through App hydration.** A strict real-App check reproduced $99.0M estimated opening receipts against $96.4M canonical paid receipts. The old App normalizer dropped financial fields, booking costs, signed beneficiary IDs and cinema metadata. Hydration now preserves the release before applying defaults. Existing fully tracked theatrical mirrors can recover from their canonical ledger, including actual investor payouts; unknown legacy history is left unknown. No cash or payment record is rewritten. Both the browser failure and an actual investor-backed missing/zeroed-mirror regression are retained.

All behavioral fixes have failing-before/passing-after evidence in `evidence/`. Fixed distributor supply, paid-show allocation, country/economy budgets and the common money-settlement owner remain in use.

## Scope and reproduction

The financial fixture explicitly authors an existing **$3B advanced studio**, completed craft packages and top-eight actual catalogue cast. It does not grant any precursor gross or audience: the four films earn those in the engine. Production funding and campaigns are debited, the engine creates each premiere from `AWAITING_RELEASE`, and all tickets are paid through the common market. This does not verify acquiring that capital/cast/craft from a new $2,000 career.

The long financial soak does not manage ordinary life choices and can retain a deceased owner's flags while continuing economic simulation. It is an engine/economy test, not a complete playable life journey. The separate App fixture explicitly starts a healthy advanced owner and clears unresolved life/award prompts, retaining an exact hash of all economic state. Its final film starts at **zero gross**; actual App Age Ups, money settlement and reload are verified separately. This control must not be presented as ordinary-career survival proof.

The [compressed earned checkpoint](evidence/earned-owner.json.gz) is authored test data, not a real user's save.
