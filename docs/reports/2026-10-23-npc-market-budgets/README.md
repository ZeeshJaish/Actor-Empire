## What changed

- Independent NPC movie admission and final greenlight now quote the reachable paying market: country/language access, participation, household cinema wallets, canonical distributor prices and producer shares, campaign saturation, existing/upcoming competition and earned universe familiarity. Missing legacy overlays remain unknown; known zero demand cannot authorize a new liability.
- An unsigned standalone/founding package can develop a smaller casting scope, retaining the corresponding creative budget curve. All newly underwritten movie packages persist an optional scope version so actual actor selection respects the target used in the quote. Existing greenlights, selected casts, signed commissions, series and player-controlled packages retain their existing rules.
- Unpaid production and campaign commitments plus operating runway protect studio cash. A studio can risk 0.1–0.4% of uncommitted cash, based on existing risk tolerance, on a small uncertain project with real demand and a minimum receipts case. This is a planning limit, not a cash grant or an earning multiplier. A forecast need not predict a guaranteed winner.
- The live audience/revenue kernel, payment authority and historical receipts are unchanged. No hard public-gross ceiling was added. A scaled supported-market test can fund more than $300M; this is an algorithmic no-fixed-ceiling check, not a promise that every ordinary studio can currently fund that package.
