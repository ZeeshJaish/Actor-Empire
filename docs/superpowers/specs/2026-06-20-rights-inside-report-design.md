# Rights Market Phase 2: The Inside Report

## Goal

Turn public rights listings into informed, suspenseful studio decisions without adding a new app or dashboard.

## Market Structure

- The Development Lab Market has two lanes: `Scripts` and `Properties`.
- `Scripts` includes finished screenplays and adaptable source material.
- `Properties` contains characters, franchises, catalogs, and story worlds.

## Investigation Loop

1. Open a public market file.
2. Choose `Get Inside Report`.
3. Pay a rarity-based investigation cost.
4. Wait one to three game weeks.
5. Receive an unread Studio Intelligence inbox message.
6. Open the completed report in the Properties market.
7. Choose `Acquire` or `Walk Away`.

The studio may investigate at most two opportunities at once. Starting an
investigation automatically tracks the opportunity. Active investigations get
a short due-diligence extension so a listing cannot expire before its report is
delivered.

## Report Contents

The deterministic report reveals:

- estimated value range
- audience loyalty
- commercial potential
- ownership and legal risk
- rival activity
- recommended format
- one hidden advantage
- one hidden danger

The same opportunity always produces the same report. Reloading or reopening
the screen cannot reroll it.

## Decisions

- `Acquire`: opens the Deal Room with better negotiating context from the report.
- `Walk Away`: dismisses the opportunity for the current market cycle with no refund.

Investigation is optional. Publicly listed properties can still be acquired
blind from the market file, but the report gives better decision quality and
negotiation leverage.

## Presentation

Public information keeps the existing black-and-amber industry board. A
finished report opens as a tactile confidential intelligence packet with a
seal, reveal animation, signal meters, and clear decision controls. Motion is
reduced when the player requests reduced motion.

## Save Safety

All new fields are optional and normalized. Existing rights listings default to
an uninvestigated state.
