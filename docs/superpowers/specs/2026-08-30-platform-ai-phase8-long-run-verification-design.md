# Platform AI Phase 8 Long-Run Verification Design

## Purpose

Phase 8 proves that the completed Platform AI and streaming ecosystem remain
deterministic, financially explainable, competitive, observable, and bounded
through 10-, 25-, and 50-year simulations. It adds no hidden gameplay aid.

## Canonical simulation seam

The endurance harness must process rival streaming through
`processStreamingIndustryWorldWeek`. Calling `processPlatformAiWorldTurn`
directly is no longer representative because it bypasses regional/generated
operators and their news.

Each simulated week must:

1. Set the player calendar to the explicit absolute week.
2. Process core Platform AI, then the wider streaming ecosystem.
3. Merge material events into the existing bounded News history.
4. Preserve deterministic IDs and make same-week replay a no-op.
5. Observe canonical projects, rights, productions, finances, awards, news,
   and ecosystem state.

## Horizons and regimes

The canonical checkpoints are 520, 1,300, and 2,600 weeks. Multiple baseline,
lean, and adverse deterministic fixtures must reach the final horizon. The
same run reports each checkpoint rather than substituting three unrelated
short simulations.

## Required observations

Per core platform: cash, debt, valuation, subscribers, releases, hits, solid
results, flops, cancellations, distress, rescues, research growth, market
withdrawals, maximum concurrent productions, awards, commercial wins, and
ownership handoff.

Per ecosystem: generated launches, promotions, distress/recovery events,
closures, active generated population, normalized market shares, and each
operator's weekly checkpoint.

Global: duplicate projects, invalid theatrical originals, dangling material
references, duplicate news IDs, news date errors, maximum news history, save
size, and runtime.

## Balance and integrity

Aggregate hit rate targets 35–55 percent and clear flops 10–25 percent.
Competence should improve pooled outcomes without producing a universal
winner. Adverse fixtures must create distress, and rescue cannot be automatic.

Every numeric value must remain finite. Money must reconcile through finance
snapshots. No company receives hidden cash, guaranteed quality, silent rescue,
or infinite parent support. Player acquisition stops AI mutation immediately.

## Persistence and scalability

A JSON save/migration midpoint must begin from the exact same canonical state
and produce the same subsequent result. Histories and saves remain bounded
without removing active rights, productions, payments, or legal references.

The endurance runner must expose progress and complete in a practical bounded
runtime. Performance changes may optimize repeated validation and observation,
but may not skip a canonical weekly decision or weaken invariants.

## News boundary

Phase 8 verifies the existing News feed: material platform and ecosystem events
appear once, use the correct date, and remain bounded. Routine accounting stays
in diagnostics. The postponed X/Instagram reaction-template project remains
outside this phase.

## Completion

Phase 8 completes only after all fixtures reach 2,600 weeks, balance bands and
invariants pass, midpoint determinism passes, focused regressions and production
build pass, and a saved report records results and remaining future work.
