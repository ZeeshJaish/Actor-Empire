# Platform AI Phase 3: Player Production Commissions

## Scope

Phase 3 connects AI streaming-platform commissioning to the player's existing Production House. It does not create a parallel production minigame and it does not automatically release delivered work.

## Player flow

1. An AI platform creates a production brief and may offer it to a player-owned studio.
2. The offer arrives in Phone > Messages with the genre, format, fixed production cap, producer fee, minimum IMDb expectation, and a fair delivery allowance.
3. Accepting the offer creates a commission-reference tile, such as `Netflix Commission #202`, in that studio's active Production House slate. It is marked `SCRIPT REQUIRED`; it is not yet a named film.
4. Opening the tile starts the normal Greenlight workflow at script selection. The chosen script must match the commissioned format and genre. If none exists, the player can visit the Script Marketplace or start a matching Development Lab concept and return later.
5. The player hires the director, cast, crew, equipment, and locations through the existing Greenlight screens.
6. The platform cap is the only production financing. Investors, studio top-ups, and release-marketing spend are unavailable for the commissioned package. If the package costs too much, the player must reduce it.
7. At Greenlight, the selected script title becomes the production title while the commission reference remains contract metadata. Greenlight creates one normal studio commitment linked to one canonical industry production. Existing career activities and the existing production-crisis system then operate on it.
8. Delivery pays the remaining producer fee and compares the persisted final quality with the stated rating expectation. The platform relationship changes, but delivery does not create a release.

## Economics

- The production budget is pass-through money and cannot become studio profit.
- The player's earnings are a separate producer fee, calculated deterministically from the production cap, studio track record, and platform relationship.
- Twenty-five percent of the fee is paid at Greenlight and seventy-five percent at delivery.
- Producer-fee milestones count as studio revenue and profit. Restricted production funds never count as revenue.
- Unused production money returns to the commissioning platform when the package is greenlit.
- Cancelling or transferring a commission forfeits unpaid fee and applies relationship/cooldown consequences.

## Timing

The current Production House calendar takes roughly 24-35 weeks. Phase 3 therefore grants a safe allowance of at least 40 weeks, or the generated calendar plus six weeks when that is longer. The deadline is recorded for visibility and the future Law Pack, but this phase applies no late-delivery penalty.

## Talent

The existing three-week Greenlight talent-pool refresh remains the availability mechanic. Canonical bookings remain useful history, but overlapping NPC actor/director work is allowed and cannot block Greenlight or AI commissions. No NPC overwork quality penalty and no talent-replacement workflow are added in this phase.

## Quality and relationship

Final delivery quality uses the project's already-persisted package quality and career/production execution. It is never rerolled at delivery. Meeting the target improves trust; two successful deliveries make future offers more likely. A small miss causes a small relationship loss. A severe miss or abandoned commission causes a larger loss, a 15-30 week cooldown, and visible news/message consequences.

## Presentation and ownership boundary

- `NETFLIX COMMISSION` or the equivalent platform label is a permanent source marker shown separately from the changing phase badge.
- The phase badge remains free to show `SCRIPT REQUIRED`, `PRE-PRODUCTION`, `PRODUCTION`, `POST-PRODUCTION`, or `DELIVERED` without clashing with source, IMDb, outcome, or award badges.
- Delivered commissions appear as display-only production credits in Studio Filmography.
- Commission credits do not enter owned-film counts, average IMDb, hit/flop results, box office, library valuation, franchise ownership, or owned-catalogue prestige.
- Greenlight displays the live production package against the fixed cap and keeps the producer fee visually separate.

## Deferred

- Lawsuits, enforceable late-delivery damages, and actor/director breach claims.
- Talent replacement and firing.
- Direct actor-database search and multi-week talent offer letters.
- Co-producer recovery for player platform commissions.
- Localization, scheduling, release, windowing, and performance settlement remain later phases.
