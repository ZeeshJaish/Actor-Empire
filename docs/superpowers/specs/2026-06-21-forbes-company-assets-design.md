# Forbes Company Assets — Phase 5 Slice 2 Design

## Goal

Extend the existing Forbes studio profile with the strategic assets that explain what a company actually owns and can produce.

## Scope

The profile will show rights/IP, franchises, universes, facilities and key talent. Management personality and ownership remain in the existing Company Identity section. This slice does not add offers, purchases, auctions or investment actions.

## Data Strategy

Player studios use their actual owned rights, released franchises, universes, facility upgrades, staff and talent contracts. NPC ventures use their real project history, founder and current market data. Established market studios use existing projects, universes and recurring talent first; missing infrastructure is filled with stable archetype-based Forbes estimates and explicitly labelled as estimated.

## UI

Add a compact Strategic Assets section after Performance Record. Four command cells summarize IP, franchises, universes and facilities. A Key Talent strip shows up to four names with their relationship to the studio. Expanded lists are avoided so the dossier stays readable on mobile.

## Testing

Extend the pure profile audit to verify actual player assets, project-derived franchises, universe ownership, facilities and talent. Extend the UI audit for the new section labels. Verify the rendered player, market and NPC profiles in the in-app browser.
