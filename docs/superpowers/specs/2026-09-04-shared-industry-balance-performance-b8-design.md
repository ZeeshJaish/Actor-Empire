# Project B Phase B8 — Shared Industry Balance, Performance, and Long-Run Certification Design

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Programme position:** Final phase of Project B  
**Status:** User-approved design; implementation plan pending specification review

## Purpose

B8 proves that the complete shared entertainment industry remains competitive, varied, explainable, deterministic, save-safe, and responsive across multi-generation careers. It certifies the combined B1–B7 system and tunes demonstrated balance or performance failures without reducing simulation depth.

B8 is not a new management feature. It adds test infrastructure, approved balance bands, targeted corrections, and a final Shared Industry Intelligence report. The player-facing result is a faster and more believable long-term game rather than another screen or recurring task.

## Product promise

The completed system must feel like a living entertainment industry for as long as the player continues a dynasty:

- established giants remain formidable without becoming immortal;
- small specialists and regional companies can create breakout successes;
- new studios and platforms launch at different credible potential levels;
- companies can grow, stagnate, receive disclosed funding, restructure, be acquired, merge, close, or fail;
- strong resources improve opportunity but never guarantee project quality;
- public projects have durable development, production, ownership, release, economic, rights, and universe lineage;
- public surfaces agree about what happened; and
- mature Process Week and save operations remain practical on mobile-oriented hardware.

No B8 optimization may delete a canonical outcome, skip a due decision, weaken a financial obligation, or replace the real simulation with a fake reporting model.

## Existing systems reused

B8 exercises the systems players actually use rather than creating a parallel certification engine:

- B1 Studio AI company foundation, control, finance, capacity, and lifecycle.
- B2 shared statistical intelligence, scheduled decisions, learning, and deterministic scoring.

`WorldState` and its existing canonical registries remain authoritative. B8 metrics observe those registries; they do not become a second source of truth.

## Certification architecture

### Hybrid evidence model

B8 uses three complementary layers.

1. **Canonical shared-industry endurance:** the real B7 entered-week coordinator runs the B1–B7 industry and Project A integration for every simulated week. It reaches the complete 20,800-week horizon.
2. **Full Process Week checkpoints:** representative player saves run the actual `processGameWeek` pipeline, persistence preparation, structured-clone proxy, trusted UI commit, and reload checks. This captures player-only systems that the industry runner intentionally does not need to execute 20,800 times.
3. **Browser gameplay inspection:** a mature save is played through representative interactions to verify immediate processing feedback, coherent public surfaces, manageable event volume, acquisition continuity, and recovery after processing failure.

Component-only 400-year audits remain useful regressions but cannot certify B8 alone. Conversely, repeating every player animation and private UI calculation for five complete 400-year worlds would waste engineering time without increasing simulation coverage. The hybrid model must prove that the canonical seams between both layers remain equivalent.

### Determinism

Every scenario has a stable seed and explicit absolute-week input. IDs, decisions, project outcomes, company lifecycle transitions, publications, rights changes, and financial movements must be replayable.

The runner records deterministic digests at every checkpoint. A replay from the same initial state and seed must produce the same digest. A midpoint JSON save, migration, and continuation must match the uninterrupted branch.

No canonical path may depend on an unpersisted `Date.now()`, `Math.random()`, iteration-order accident, or random identifier.

## Scenario matrix

Five deterministic regimes run through 520, 1,300, 2,600, 5,200, and 20,800 weeks—10, 25, 50, 100, and 400 years.

### Baseline

A healthy but contested market with ordinary demand, access to capital, and mixed company competence. It establishes the central expected ranges.

### Lean

Lower demand, tighter financing, reduced acquisition appetite, and stronger pressure on weak projects. Companies must reduce spending, delay, restructure, or close without receiving hidden cash.

### Boom

Subscriber growth, investment, market expansion, premium bidding, and greater project ambition. The economy must create opportunity without turning every release into a hit or allowing uncontrolled inflation.

### Crowded

More active studios and platforms, talent congestion, content saturation, rights competition, and bidding pressure. Competition must produce strategic differentiation rather than identical project spam.

### Adverse

Expensive failures, churn, weak demand, distressed companies, and limited rescue capital. It proves that closure and loss are possible while preventing the entire industry from collapsing permanently.

Generated entrants use existing company-generation rules. They may enter with weak, ordinary, strong, or exceptional potential when a persisted funding and competence explanation supports it. No entrant is required to begin at level zero.

## Metrics model

The B8 report records checkpoint and final-horizon metrics without storing audit-only telemetry in player saves.

### Company and economy

- launches, promotions, maturity, distress, recovery, external funding, restructuring, acquisition, merger, dormancy, closure, and bankruptcy;
- cash, debt, valuation, revenue, operating cost, production commitments, rights commitments, subscriber economics, runway, and reconciled external capital;
- survival by company tier and archetype;
- yearly commercial, subscriber, box-office, prestige, and awards leaders; and
- dominance share, including consecutive and total winning years.

Every material cash increase must reconcile to revenue, settlement, borrowing, equity investment, sale, or another persisted source. AI-only cost advantages apply only while the company remains AI-controlled and end immediately on player acquisition.

### Project pipeline

- concepts admitted, rewrites, holds, turnarounds, greenlights, commissions, productions, delays, overruns, cancellations, sales, releases, hits, solid outcomes, and flops;
- development and production cadence;
- planned versus actual budget and duration;
- final quality distribution by competence and budget suitability;
- theatrical, streaming-only, and hybrid release routing;
- project, production, studio, platform, rights, talent, and universe reference integrity; and
- studio relationship changes following successful or failed commissioned work.

### Content diversity

- format, genre, subgenre, tone, theme, setting, period, audience, language, market, release path, source intent, and budget-tier distribution;
- exact fingerprint repetition and near-repeat pressure;
- title collision rate;
- novelty-score distribution;
- standalone, sequel, spin-off, reboot, and shared-universe frequency;
- universe creation, continuation, decline, dormancy, revival, and ending; and
- concentration of talent, genres, franchises, and projects by company.

### Streaming, bidding, and rights

- subscriber growth, churn, engagement, ARPU, catalogue strength, research, localization, market expansion, and infrastructure health;
- bidding participation, offer composition, revision, closure, acceptance, and remaining-market eligibility;
- commission creation, acceptance, delivery, quality performance, cooldown, and relationship effects;

### Public-world coherence

- News, X, and Instagram material-event cadence;

- stable canonical identity across News, IMDb, awards, Box Office, Forbes evidence, rights records, and project history;

### Technical safety

- runtime by week, year, checkpoint, scenario, and major processing stage;

- heap observations where the runtime exposes them;
- duplicate IDs, dangling references, non-finite values, negative impossible balances, and same-week double processing;

- acquisition, reverse sale, subsidiary delegation, dynasty transition, and retired-family project continuity.

## Approved balance principles and bands

B8 begins with broad safety bands. Baseline measurements may lead to narrower final bands, but a band may only be changed with a documented gameplay reason—not merely to make a failing audit green.

### Outcome diversity

- Each long-lived company must record both successes and failures over a 100-year run.
- No company may win more than 65 percent of measured commercial, subscriber, box-office, or awards years in the baseline aggregate.
- At least one small or regional company must achieve a breakout result in the baseline, boom, or crowded matrix.
- At least one major company must suffer an expensive failure in every complete matrix.
- Competence must improve pooled expected outcomes while preserving overlapping distributions between strong and weak companies.
- No project receives a guaranteed quality floor from money, fame, or competence.

### Company lifecycle

- Baseline must retain meaningful established competition at 50, 100, and 400 years.
- Lean and adverse regimes must produce distress and at least one genuine closure or acquisition.
- Boom and crowded regimes must admit credible new challengers.
- The adverse regime must retain or regenerate enough viable competition to avoid permanent monopoly or empty markets.
- Rescue funding is finite, recorded, and probabilistic. Repeated rescue cannot guarantee survival.
- No company may survive solely through unexplained passive cash drift.

### Variety and cadence

- Duplicate canonical IDs and exact duplicate public titles are zero.
- Exact content-fingerprint repetition outside explicit sequel/universe lineage is zero.
- Near-repeat concentration, genre dominance, franchise load, and talent load must remain below final limits derived from baseline distributions and documented in the report.
- Material public events must be neither permanently silent nor weekly accounting spam. Final annual cadence bands are calibrated from actual matrix output and then frozen in the verifier.

### Integrity

- `NaN`, positive infinity, and negative infinity occurrences are zero.
- Broken material references, duplicated payments, duplicated rights, double-advanced weeks, and theatrical leakage from streaming-only projects are zero.
- Save/reload and uninterrupted branches produce identical canonical digests.
- Acquired companies receive player-standard cost and time from the acquisition week onward.

## Player-experience certification

B8 treats player experience as an observable contract.

### Process Week

- The processing indicator must be paintable within 100 ms of the tap in the browser lab.
- The re-entry lock must prevent double advancement while the interface yields for paint.
- The interface must recover cleanly after both success and injected processing failure.
- Ordinary mature weeks should not feel frozen, and annual/save-heavy weeks must remain visibly processing rather than appearing unresponsive.

### Public information

- News and social surfaces explain material changes without exposing internal AI status enums or decision scores.
- Forbes reflects performance through observable financial and market signals.
- Box Office, IMDb, awards, rights history, and project details tell one consistent story.
- Players can inspect durable ancestral projects without turning the Legacy section into a complete internal transaction log.

### Management load

- B8 adds no mandatory player decisions.
- Existing Strategy, Custom, Full Control, manual subsidiary, board-review, and automatic subsidiary modes remain the complexity controls.
- Rival-company depth stays primarily statistical until ownership or a player-facing negotiation materializes it into detailed gameplay.

## Performance budgets

All timing claims must name the machine/runtime, save fixture, sample count, cache state, and whether the result is measured or derived.

### Mature Process Week

Using the existing age-82 composite fixture updated for B1–B7:

- at least one warm-up plus 20 consecutive measured real `processGameWeek` runs;
- desktop complete-path p95 at or below 500 ms;
- annual/save-heavy desktop p95 at or below 750 ms;
- browser-visible processing feedback within 100 ms;
- four-times CPU-time proxy at or below 2,000 ms for ordinary mature weeks and 3,000 ms for heavy save weeks; and
- zero input mutations and exactly one week advance per successful call.

The four-times number is a proxy, not physical-device evidence. If no low-end Android is connected, physical Tecno, Redmi, Samsung, or equivalent performance remains explicitly unverified.

### Long-run engineering runtime

- Each canonical 400-year scenario reports progress at bounded intervals.
- Runtime may not grow without bound as terminal histories accumulate.
- The complete five-scenario certification must finish in practical engineering time on the reference machine. The implementation plan will establish a measured baseline before freezing a numeric wall-clock gate.
- Component replays may use parallel workers, but one scenario's canonical weekly sequence remains ordered.

### Save growth

- Active obligations and permanent public history are never pruned to satisfy a byte target.
- Private proposals, processed keys, forecasts, transient observations, and terminal execution detail use existing bounded summaries.
- Checkpoint bytes must demonstrate convergence toward bounded growth rather than linear accumulation across the 100- to 400-year interval.
- Existing full-local-mirror and authoritative IndexedDB boundaries remain intact.

## Tuning policy

B8 applies changes only after a focused audit demonstrates a failure.

The permitted order is:

1. eliminate duplicate processing and repeated canonical-registry scans;
2. reuse deterministic turn-local indexes;
3. ensure scheduled AI lanes execute only when due;
4. compact terminal private workflow into existing permanent summaries;
5. tune disclosed economic, cadence, quality, rescue, or lifecycle coefficients when observed distributions violate the approved experience principles; and
6. introduce a worker boundary only if measured main-thread computation still misses the responsiveness budget.

Every gameplay tuning change requires:

- a RED audit demonstrating the unwanted distribution or invariant failure;
- a short explanation of why the previous behaviour harmed gameplay;
- a minimal coefficient or logic correction;
- GREEN focused and adjacent regression audits; and
- a fresh matrix measurement rather than an assumed improvement.

B8 must not lower simulation population, remove canonical releases, skip due decisions, suppress legitimate failure, or delete permanent history solely to improve runtime.

## Browser and gameplay inspection

The final browser pass uses a verified local Actor Empire server and a mature deterministic save. It covers:

- Process Week visible feedback and re-entry lock;
- News, X, Instagram, IMDb, Box Office, Forbes, awards, and rights coherence;
- a commission, bidding-room eligibility explanation, rights expiry/remaining-market case, and subsidiary mode;
- acquisition of an active company and inspection of inherited work;
- at least several consecutive processed weeks to observe event spacing; and
- console errors and visible failure recovery.

Browser observations are qualitative evidence and do not replace deterministic audits.

## Implementation stages

### Stage 1 — Certification contracts and scenario fixtures

Define typed scenarios, checkpoints, approved invariant categories, deterministic seeds, metric schemas, digest rules, and fixture construction by reusing existing initializers and migrations.

### Stage 2 — Canonical 400-year runner

Run the B7 coordinator and canonical Project A dependencies through all checkpoints. Add progress output, bounded metric aggregation, midpoint resume, and deterministic replay.

### Stage 3 — Gameplay-experience verifier

Measure content variety, company competition, universe behaviour, public-event cadence, project lineage, and cross-surface identity. Establish the initial baseline, then freeze justified bands.

### Stage 4 — Economy and lifecycle tuning

Correct only demonstrated failures in finance, rescue, company turnover, entrant strength, dominance, project cadence, quality distribution, or outcome diversity.

### Stage 5 — Save, ownership, and dynasty parity

Verify migration, reload, recovery, acquisition, reverse sale, subsidiary modes, and multi-generation history under the combined system.

### Stage 6 — Process Week and save-growth performance

Refresh the age-82 fixture for B1–B7, measure the real complete path, profile slow stages, and apply evidence-led optimizations without outcome changes.

### Stage 7 — Full regression and final report

Run the five-scenario matrix, full Process Week benchmark, B1–B7 and Project A aggregates, build, diff check, and browser inspection. Record exact results, unavailable device evidence, remaining diagnostics, and deferred work.

## Verification requirements

B8 cannot complete from a partial or interrupted endurance run.

Required evidence:

- all five scenarios reach 20,800 weeks;
- every checkpoint metric and frozen band passes;
- deterministic replay and midpoint save/reload pass;
- old-save migration, recovery, dynasty, acquisition, sale, and subsidiary parity pass;
- Process Week and save-growth budgets pass under recorded conditions;
- B1–B7 focused and aggregate audits pass;
- Project A rights, bidding, commission, catalogue, transfer, expiry, and Rights Office regressions pass;
- player Production House, Streaming House, career, and weekly safety checks pass;
- production build and `git diff --check` pass;
- browser gameplay inspection passes; and
- the final Shared Industry Intelligence report is reviewed by the user.

Repository-wide diagnostics that are demonstrably unrelated baseline failures may be reported separately, but no B8-owned diagnostic may remain.

## Deferred boundary

B8 verifies the restrained B7 event projection and its cadence. It does not implement Project C's deeper media personalities, fandoms, rumours, replies, publication voices, or time-separated melodrama templates.

It also does not add a player-facing debug dashboard or expose internal AI states. Audit reports and developer scripts own certification telemetry.

## Completion gate

Project B completes only when:

- the five canonical 400-year scenarios finish in practical engineering time;
- approved competition, economy, variety, cadence, integrity, runtime, and save-growth bands pass;
- save/reload, migration, dynasty, acquisition, sale, and subsidiary parity pass;
- no canonical path contains unpersisted randomness, hidden passive cash, unbounded private history, duplicate records, or unexplained public projects;
- mature Process Week remains responsive under the recorded desktop and proxy budgets;
- existing Project A and player gameplay remain intact;
- the final report documents implementation, tuning, performance, limitations, and deferred work; and
- the user approves the final report.

After this gate, B1–B8 and Project B are complete. Project C — Shared Industry Reactions and Media World — becomes the next approval-gated roadmap project.
