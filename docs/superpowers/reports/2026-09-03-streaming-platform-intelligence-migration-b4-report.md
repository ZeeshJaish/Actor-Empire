# Project B Phase B4 Completion Report — Streaming Platform AI Migration

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Status:** Complete  
**Next approval gate:** B5 — Production Studio Slate and Greenlight Simulation

## Outcome

B4 makes B2 statistical proposals and linked B3 content fingerprints the authoritative private decision source for AI-controlled streaming platforms. Existing domain services remain authoritative for every real gameplay mutation.

The live flagship turn no longer runs the former unconditional catalogue/research/market planning cycle. It progresses existing commitments, receives newly due intelligence proposals, converts each proposal once into a deterministic intent, and sends that intent through the appropriate existing validator and executor.

## Simulation changes

- Content proposals retain the exact B3 fingerprint that won selection.
- A pure intent adapter maps proposal and fingerprint facts into original commission, released-title licence, catalogue acquisition, technology research, localization research, exact market entry, localization work, or hold.
- Affordability is capped by the proposal and canonical platform finances; ambition cannot mint spending capacity.
- Content sourcing reads the B3 format, genre, audience, language, source route, budget curve, market priority, and universe relationship.
- External-rights routes inspect released projects only when such a proposal is due. An impossible rights route fails instead of silently becoming an original.
- Successful commitments mark their fingerprint committed and retain canonical lineage.
- Processed proposal keys and outcomes are deterministic and bounded to 64 and 32 records per flagship platform.
- Player acquisition immediately stops rival intent execution while preserving completed commitments and ongoing canonical work.

## Existing systems reused

B4 does not create replacement authorities. It reuses:

- Platform AI content planning, commitment, production, release, and player-studio commission services;
- the Project A rights registry, scope validation, contracts, bidding, transfers, and settlement;
- the existing research portfolio, dependencies, affordability, and commitment rules;
- exact market-entry and localization requirement/job services;
- existing platform cash, debt, subscribers, valuation, distress, recapitalization, dormancy, acquisition, and controller logic;
- the streaming ecosystem's existing lightweight weekly finance and market-share simulation; and
- the current save normalization, compaction, integrity, and acquisition handoff boundaries.

## Regional and generated platforms

Every non-core streaming operator now receives its own bounded B2/B3 private intelligence state, derived from its current finance, audience, catalogue, technology, localization, market presence, risk, brand, prestige, and lifecycle. Only due lanes run.

Their existing lightweight ecosystem economy remains authoritative. A new fail-closed bidder bridge explains why an operator cannot cross into the canonical rights market: inactive lifecycle, missing identity, unavailable territory, inadequate runway, or unsupported settlement identity. This prevents a privately simulated operator from inventing rights or money.

## Player-visible result

B4 adds no raw AI-status dashboard and no new management chore. Its consequences arrive through existing game surfaces:

- platform commission messages;
- bidding-room participation and offers;
- catalogue and rights changes;
- technology, localization, and country expansion results;
- original productions and releases;
- subscriber, finance, distress, valuation, and Forbes movement; and
- existing canonical event/news hooks.

The bidding-room clock, equal response cooldown, extensions, revisions, final offers, withdrawals, contract terms, territorial exclusions, and settlement behavior are unchanged.

## Persistence and migration

Old platform states normalize a B4 migration ledger without deleting platform history. The ledger sits inside the existing industry-intelligence state so B2/B3 normalization, save protection, and compaction continue to apply. Wider ecosystem operators also normalize intelligence in place. Unsupported operators remain private simulation participants until a later canonical identity/settlement bridge explicitly supports them.
