# Project C Phase C7 Completion Report

## Outcome

C7 turns individual industry stories into durable, evidence-backed media memory. Important canonical facts can now build or contradict a saved narrative, recurring media personalities remember meaningful interactions, and public feuds require repeated conflict across distinct entered weeks rather than one bad headline.

The existing publicist is now a passive team benefit. It automatically amplifies bounded positive media effects and mitigates bounded negative effects within tier capacity, records what it changed, and never reverses the sign or rewrites the named outcome. There is no recommendation chore, custom control mode, or automatic player post.

## Player-visible result

- News, X, Instagram, and industry YouTube detail views can show the relevant long-term narrative and its latest evidence.
- X media profiles can show a personality's remembered relationship or qualified feud with the player.
- The Team publicist view shows concrete current-week after-action work such as extra reach or prevented damage.
- Eligible X, Instagram, YouTube, Career promotion, and press actions can be linked to one active project. Only that project's existing promotional buzz changes.
- Repeated promotion of the same project has diminishing returns. Lifestyle posts remain project-neutral.
- Narrative retrospectives can appear in News and X only after new evidence and inside the bounded eight-week cooldown.

## Existing systems reused

- B7 remains the only authority for canonical industry facts.
- C1 stories provide shared subject and evidence lineage.
- C2 institutions and recurring personalities remain the author and identity layer.
- C3 response outcomes and C6 resolved claims feed relationship memory without being reapplied.
- C4 YouTube and C5 Instagram/fandom presentation reuse the same narrative context.
- Existing commitment `promotionalBuzz`, Career promotion, press conference, social composer, energy cost, and Release Wizard paid-marketing ownership remain canonical.
- Existing Team members and publicist salaries remain the full recurring PR cost.

## Persistence and compatibility

Industry-media schema is now 7 and save migration is 40. Old saves receive empty C7 collections; migration does not fabricate historical narratives, feuds, interventions, or project promotion records. Normalization clamps malformed values, removes invalid references, deduplicates exact-once keys, and keeps bounded collections: 120 narratives, 8 landmarks each, 240 relationships, 12 relationship landmarks/conflict weeks, 160 PR interventions, 160 promotion attributions, and 640 C7 keys.

## Safety boundaries

C7 cannot change canonical quality, IMDb ratings, reviews, awards, rights, contracts, production, ownership, revenue, or whether a response backfired or a claim was refuted. Publicist effects cannot turn damage into a reward. A bad press appearance may still reduce project buzz; PR can only reduce the damage. Silence remains safe.
