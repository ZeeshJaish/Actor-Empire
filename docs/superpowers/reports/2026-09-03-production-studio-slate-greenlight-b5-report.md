# Project B Phase B5 Completion Report — Production Studio Slate and Greenlight Simulation

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Status:** Complete
**Next approval gate:** B6 — AI Production, Release, and Commercial Results

## Outcome

B5 replaces independent instant NPC-studio project decisions with deterministic, bounded development slates. AI studios now act from due B2 proposals and linked B3 fingerprints, pay exact internal development costs, review projects over time, and resolve greenlight, rewrite, hold, turnaround, or abandonment decisions from their real company condition.

B5 stops at greenlight. It does not implement B6 casting, physical-production incidents, release strategy, box office, streaming performance, or awards.

## Simulation changes

- Every new independent studio intention requires one newly due B2 content proposal and its selected B3 fingerprint.
- Admission checks controller, lifecycle, ownership, duplicate proposal/fingerprint identity, development capacity, runway, affordability, and external-right feasibility.
- Selected projects persist one compact development commitment rather than a private full script or rejected candidate pool.
- Exact development and rewrite payments use the B1 studio ledger and are idempotent.
- Review timing and results use studio strategy, competence, reputation, cash, runway, capacity, fingerprint novelty, budget suitability, fatigue, proposal forecasts, and persisted uncertainty.
- Greenlights freeze their budget and reserve production capacity. Handoff releases that reservation without inventing cancellation or commercial success.
- Active capacity counts are derived from normalized slate records, preventing stale counters and phantom capacity.
- AI commissions already materialized in `industryProductions` are adopted as handed-off slate commitments without charging independent development money or changing platform funding/fees.
- Player-controlled and terminal studios receive no rival slate mutation.

## Legacy transition

The generated-venture scheduler can still launch companies and provide its compatibility projection. It can no longer originate a project merely because `nextProjectWeek` arrived.

Its temporary B5-to-B6 release bridge must consume one unique independent `GREENLIT` commitment. The materialized project receives stable B5 commitment and B3 fingerprint lineage, and the same greenlight cannot be consumed twice. B6 will replace this remaining instant production/release bridge.

## Persistence and performance

- Each studio retains at most 64 processed proposal keys and 64 processed review keys.
- Terminal/recent slate history is capped at 24 records while active work is never evicted.
- Only due intelligence lanes build full decision context; non-due weeks skip that material work.
- Rejected candidate pools and private scripts are not stored.
- Old saves normalize an empty B5 slate at their current week, so historical B2 proposals are not replayed.

The deterministic 20,800-week B5 run covered four representative studio profiles and was replayed exactly.
