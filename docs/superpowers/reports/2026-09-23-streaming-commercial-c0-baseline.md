# Streaming commercial C0 baseline

Date: 2026-09-23

Baseline commit: `f24dc36` (`codex/rights-market-phase1`)

Scope: diagnostic fixtures and reporting only; no gameplay, pricing, AI, save, or balance rule changed

## Decision-level finding

The intended business-model trade-off is **not yet proven in the live game**. The existing commercial formula is finite and can produce advertising income from delivered eligible hours. However, an ad-only player fixture seeded with 250,000 free-access accounts delivers 347,977 title hours, then settles **$0 advertising revenue**. The free-access `OPEN_ACCESS` plan is generated by `worldStreamingOffers.ts`, but the player viewing stage looks for its ID in the saved pricing plans, where an ad-only platform has no plan. This makes its ad-eligible share zero (`worldStreamingViewing.ts`). The C0 probe deliberately pins this inherited defect; it does not repair it.

Likewise, all 33 AI rivals in the current mature-field fixture include subscriptions, ads, and rentals. The AI configuration generator always starts with `subs`, so a genuinely ad-only or rental-only rival does not yet exist. AI participation in the shared commercial formula is real, but complete three-model strategy parity is not.

## Reproducible fixture

Run `npm run audit:streaming-commercial-c0`. It runs twice and asserts identical output. Its fixed-input commercial calculation uses 150,000 viewing accounts, 600,000 ad-eligible hours, 80,000 non-subscriber opportunities, and the same title/capability/reputation inputs for every row. These are **controlled calculations, not full-world buyer or profit forecasts**. Subscription cash is booked by the separate subscription path, so the subscription row's zero *incremental* revenue must not be read as zero subscription income.

| Configuration | Current fixed-input result |
| --- | ---: |
| Subscription only | $0 incremental commercial revenue; subscription revenue excluded from this function |
| Ads only, 4 min/hour, $20 CPM | 3,826,560 filled impressions; $76,531 ad revenue; $9,184 commercial operating cost |
| Rent/buy only, $6/$20 | $8,934 rental + $4,240 purchase revenue; $461 commercial operating cost |
| Subscription + ads + rent/buy | $89,705 incremental commercial revenue; $9,645 commercial operating cost |

Ad load at fixed delivered hours and $20 CPM: 0/4/8/12 minutes per hour gives $0/$76,531/$136,226/$153,828 ad revenue. The diminishing gain from 8 to 12 minutes reflects the formula's ad-load tolerance, but the fixture holds viewing hours constant and therefore does **not** demonstrate a whole-game audience/retention trade-off. The UI currently permits 0–12 ad minutes/hour and $0–40 entered CPM (`StepPricing.tsx`). The service clamps broader input to 0–30 minutes and 0–100 CPM (`worldStreamingCommercialEconomy.ts`). The UI is an ad-minutes control, not a break-placement or ads-per-title scheduler.

At 4 minutes/hour, entered CPM $10/$20/$40 produces $38,266/$76,531/$153,062 while filled impressions stay exactly 3,826,560. A higher requested advertiser price does not reduce fill in the current formula. The $40 endpoint is therefore an unpriced upside in this controlled case; C4 must define market-realized CPM or a reserve-price/fill trade-off. This is a balance flaw, not infinite money: current outputs are bounded and finite.

## Audience and AI interpretation

The two-market launch forecast in the C0 fixture reports 2,045,631 subscription-only `subscribers` and $22,835,404.80 monthly subscription revenue. For ad-only and rent/buy-only it reports 36,624,025 and 37,036,056 `subscribers` respectively, both with $0 monthly subscription revenue. Those latter counts represent zero-price access accounts in a plan-centric forecast, **not paying subscribers, ad impressions, or rental buyers**. They cannot be used as proof of ad/store profitability. Separate free and transactional cohorts are a C3 requirement.

The mature AI fixture contains 33 rivals: 33 with `subs`, 33 with `ads`, 33 with `rentals`, 0 ad-only, and 0 rental-only. The present generator begins with `['subs']` (`worldStreamingCommercialEconomy.ts`); AI and player must move to a common offer/entitlement/choice/settlement contract in C1–C4. Different AI prices and strategies are desirable; different economic rules are not.

## Acceptance targets for later phases

1. **C1–C2:** A free account is not a paid subscriber; a rental buyer is not a recurring member. All three core-model shapes and title entitlements work for player and AI, and old saves retain historical ledger values.
2. **C3:** In the same reachable market, a free ad-led offer can attract a broader audience than a comparably attractive paid subscription, subject to catalogue, advertising tolerance, network, and rival offers. Report free viewers, subscribers, pass users, renters, purchasers, and non-converters separately; no target population is invented to force revenue.
3. **C4:** At a calibrated reference case, an engaged ad viewer earns less per viewer than a paying subscriber earns per payer, while a strong ad platform can still earn healthy total profit through scale. More ads must bring a visible audience/retention cost; a higher advertiser price must have a fill/demand cost or be market-determined. Ad-only delivered hours must produce positive revenue when filled; no ad impression exists on an ad-free pass-covered hour.
4. **Player/AI parity:** Both use the same offers, rights, customer-choice, ad-fill, cost, and cash-settlement rules. AI can choose ad-only, subscription-only, store-only, and hybrids; strategy diversity is tested without granting AI hidden free revenue.
5. **Reporting:** Forecast, weekly result, country report, AI report, and treasury agree on what is an account, who paid, what was viewed, and which revenue was earned. Do not relabel the 36–37 million free/store forecast accounts as subscribers.

C0 did not set final numeric balance targets. C3–C4 should present a sensitivity table and obtain a balance decision before changing live economics. No production settlement or historical save was modified in this phase.
